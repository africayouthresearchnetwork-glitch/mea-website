ABOUT MEA

Research grounded in evidence. Consulting shaped by insight.

MEA Research & Consulting is an independent research and consulting firm providing multidisciplinary research, evidence and analytical services to organizations across sectors.

We work at the intersection of research, data and decision-making, helping organizations understand complex challenges, generate meaningful evidence and translate findings into practical action.

Our approach combines rigorous research methods, analytical thinking and clear communication to produce evidence that can be understood, applied and used to inform better decisions.

Our approach

Rigorous Research grounded in sound methods and evidence.
Independent Objective analysis designed to support informed decisions.
Practical Insights translated into useful and actionable recommendations.
Collaborative Working with partners to understand their context and needs.
