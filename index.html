MEET THE FOUNDER

Esther Murunga

Founder & Lead Research Consultant

Esther Murunga is a Kenyan-based public health specialist and MSc One Health candidate at the University of Edinburgh, with experience in public health research, field coordination, qualitative research and evidence synthesis.

Her work brings together research methods, epidemiology, One Health and evidence-informed decision-making to explore complex public health and development challenges.

Her research experience includes coordinating adolescent mental health research with the Shamiri Institute in Murang'a County, conducting an independent qualitative study on out-of-pocket payments in maternal health financing in Makueni County, and contributing to an ongoing systematic literature review on the multisectoral control of fish-borne zoonotic parasitic infections.
